The Eight Types of Love

The presence or absence of each component — Passion, Intimacy, and Commitment — gives rise to eight distinct relationship experiences.

None
Liking
Intimacy only

Romantic Love

Companionate Love
Intimacy + Commitment
Fatuous Love

Consummate Love
